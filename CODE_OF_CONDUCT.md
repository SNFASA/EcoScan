# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

ToastTech is committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while accepting responsibility for impact.
- Welcome newcomers and explain project conventions patiently.
- Respect privacy and do not publish personal data or credentials.
- Accept maintainer decisions about project scope and moderation.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, trolling, sustained disruption, doxxing, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the contact method listed on the repository owner's GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive details. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban participants whose behaviour is harmful.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
